About RO Coirs

Connecting Global Buyers with Premium Indian Coir Products

RO Coirs is a trusted export company specializing in sourcing and supplying premium-quality coir products from reliable Indian manufacturers to customers across the globe. We focus on delivering sustainable solutions with consistent quality and dependable service.

From coco peat blocks and grow bags to coir fibre and other eco-friendly products, we ensure every shipment meets international quality standards, competitive pricing and timely delivery, building long-term partnerships with customers worldwide.

Trusted Supplier Network

We partner with verified coir manufacturers across India to source premium-quality products that meet global customer expectations.

Reliable Global Exports

We coordinate quality inspection, export documentation and worldwide logistics to ensure safe, timely and hassle-free deliveries.

Our Services

Sustainable Coir Solutions for Agriculture & Global Industries

Coco Peat Blocks

High-quality compressed coco peat blocks with excellent water retention and aeration, ideal for horticulture, hydroponics and commercial farming.

Natural Coir Fibre

Strong, eco-friendly coir fibre suitable for mattresses, erosion control, brushes, ropes, geotextiles and industrial applications.

Coir Discs

Eco-friendly compressed coir discs that expand quickly with water, making them perfect for seed germination, nurseries and home gardening.
